How this list is kept

Nothing here is copied and forgotten.

  • — Every listing links to the organiser's own page, and we re-check that page on a schedule. Each row says when it was last checked.
  • — Eligibility rules are quoted from the organiser's words. Where the wording is unclear, we say unclear rather than guessing.
  • — We never ask you to pay to apply. If an opportunity asks for a fee, report it and it comes down.
